# Project Overview

## Project name
Student Recruitment Portal

## Client
Ada College

## Pathway
Software Developer

## Core objective
Use technology to improve recruitment of T-Level students at Ada Manchester by helping prospective students and their parents/carers understand whether T-Levels are the right educational path.

## Problem being addressed
There is significant doubt around T-Levels and their effectiveness. Parents often worry that T-Levels are less valuable than A-Levels, and students do not have enough clear information to decide whether T-Levels fit their interests, skills, and career goals.

## Intended users
- Prospective students
- Parents and carers
- Schools and guidance advisors
- Ada internal stakeholders

## Core idea
Build a web app that:
- explains T-Levels clearly
- helps users compare T-Levels to other pathways
- asks structured questions to recommend whether T-Levels may be a good fit
- gives a natural-language chatbot to answer questions with evidence-based guidance
