# Product Brief

## Product statement
The Student Recruitment Portal is a web-based platform designed to help students and parents make informed decisions about T-Levels as a post-16 education option.

## Audience
- Prospective students deciding on post-16 pathways
- Parents/carers who are uncertain about T-Levels
- Schools and college advisors supporting students

## Value proposition
The platform reduces uncertainty by helping users:
- understand what T-Levels are
- see how they compare with A-Levels and apprenticeships
- assess whether T-Levels suit their interests and strengths
- ask questions in plain language and get evidence-backed answers

## User problem
Students and parents often lack clear, accessible information about T-Levels. This creates uncertainty and weak decision-making.

## Proposed solution
Create a digital experience that combines:
1. educational content about T-Levels
2. a decision-support triage flow
3. a RAG-powered chatbot for natural language questions

## Scope decision
This is not a full student portal or logged-in platform. The MVP will be a public-facing web app with no user accounts.

## Non-goals for MVP
- User authentication
- Saved user profiles
- Personal analytics dashboards
- Payment or booking flows

## Success indicator
The MVP should help a user leave with a clearer understanding of whether T-Levels could be a suitable option for them.
